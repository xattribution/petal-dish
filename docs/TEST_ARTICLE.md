# First test article

1. Export the default curved PETAL 5.2 kit. Keep one parameter set and material profile for every matching piece.
2. Print TWO copies of `FIT_TEST/seam-strip-print-two.stl`, preserving orientation. These full-width cropped petal strips reproduce the lower and upper edge print conditions and include a key and screw station. Fit their neighboring edges at the intended dish angle; do not flatten the pair onto a table. Try the key first, then the M3 × 16 screw, washers and loose nut. Check insertion, layer flare, that both seats are flat (especially the upper flange's, which prints as a short overhang), tool access and seam step before/after snugging.
3. If the key binds, correct first-layer flare/process error or increase key clearance and regenerate. Do not use bolt torque to seat it.
4. Print one full petal with a brim. Inspect layer previews first; use local slicer supports if needed. Measure the full radial and cross-petal profiles against the generated parabola. A short strip cannot reveal full-length warp.
5. Bolt the root to the hub with the M4 × 20, washers and nut. Check that the head sits below the front surface, and check the seat for crushing after tightening. If you chose inserts, first test the specified short M4 insert on a scrap with the same pilot. Inserts are a maximum of 6 mm long in a 7 mm cavity, with the mouth recessed 0.5 mm.
6. Print the hub and remaining petals only after fit/process checks pass. For staggered segments, assemble the inner ring loosely, then slide outer segments radially inward one at a time, aligning both inner neighbors in a profile jig. Cross-ring butt flanges have no projecting keys; do not force complete rings together. With staggering disabled, assemble radial strips first. Keep all screws loose. Close the final seam without force. Snug opposite seams progressively, then fit the rear hub. Record seam steps before and after each stage.
7. For a segmented dish, trial-fit a three-panel junction (two inner segments and one outer segment) before printing all rings. For the mount, test M4 hardware and washers no larger than 9 mm OD in the hub's front seats; select length from the actual stack.
8. Measure again at several elevations and after a warm hold representative of service. Check loosening, creep and seat/flange movement. Only then apply conductive finish and repeat profile/continuity checks.

No torque, wind or payload rating has been established. Begin with a restrained bench fixture and no valuable feed equipment. The foil finish can concentrate sunlight: keep it away from the Sun during handling.
